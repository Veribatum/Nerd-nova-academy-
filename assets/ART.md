Nova retains red hair, green eyes, freckles, tattoos and her established revealing pilot outfit. Sylvanas is the blonde adult elf. Samus is the adult blonde bounty hunter. Jenny is the adult silver-haired embodied AI with cybernetic arms; the inconsistent brunette variant was not used. Crew-file photographs and rewards include their established Rum Island hangar memories as well as spacecraft images.

The earlier high-collar naval command art and the cartoon SVG are not used in this edition. Attempts to generate new low-neckline portraits with the built-in image generator were rejected by the service; no failed or nonexistent generation is represented as a finished asset. This edition instead integrates the existing approved character images directly. No new image-generation prompt produced an accepted final image in this crew update.

Work mode portraits (`work-{nova,sylvanas,samus,jenny}.webp`) use the built-in image generator. The source prompt requested a realistic cinematic 2×2 crew portrait sheet in a teal starship interior, with practical opaque high-collared armor: copper-haired Nova in red and black, braided blonde elf Sylvanas in purple and charcoal, blonde Samus in blue and amber, and silver-haired Jenny in steel and cyan. Each quadrant was exported separately as WebP.

Work mode scene art uses the built-in image generator and ships as `work-nova-bridge.webp`, `work-*-mission.webp`, and `work-*-reward.webp`. Three prompts requested a wide cinematic bridge scene with armored Nova reviewing a star chart, four armored crew members working at their stations (wiring, antenna, shuttle diagnostics, and fiber patching), and four candid celebration scenes (coffee at sunrise, a salute, a repaired shuttle, and a restored module). Work mode uses one celebration scene per crew member across their unlocks.

The current Work mode crew cards, quarters, and Nova comms thumbnail use `work-*-crew.webp`. The built-in image generator prompt requested four candid, fully armored scenes aboard the Rapscallion: Nova crossing the bridge with a star chart, Sylvanas using binoculars, Samus boarding her shuttle with her helmet, and Jenny comparing circuit boards at a bench. The earlier headshot files are retained for cached clients.

The home gallery's poolside set uses `home-*.webp` from a built-in image generation prompt for retro sci-fi summer fashion illustrations of the four adult crew members aboard a starship pool deck. Nova has a sun hat and mocktail, Sylvanas a purple swimsuit and sarong at a viewport, Samus a blue-and-gold swimsuit and beach ball, and Jenny a cyan swimsuit and handheld game. Work mode selects the existing armored reward art for these entries.

The previous poolside rewards use `home-*-photo.webp`. The built-in image generator prompt requested a photographic live-action contact sheet with Nova in a red sundress at a poolside cafe, Sylvanas in a violet summer dress at the viewport, Samus playing with a beach ball in athletic resort clothes, and Jenny in a cyan resort shirt on a deck chair. The illustrated assets are retained for older cached releases.

The current Home rewards use user-supplied Rum Island images in `home-*-original.webp`. Nova is at the sunset bar, Sylvanas is in the hangar, Samus is at the beach, and Jenny appears in the Friday crew photo. These entries keep their existing unlock IDs and use armored reward art in Work mode.
